RAJASTHAN · CITY OF LAKES

Udaipur has my heart

Udaipur is my favourite place. I visited many forts and different spots around the city. I do not remember every place by name, but I still remember how beautiful the city felt. The lakes and old buildings make it special for me.

A place I would happily visit again.

Photo: tommy / Wikimedia Commons

RAJASTHAN · MOUNT ABU

A calm day by Nakki Lake

I visited Mount Abu a few months ago. I walked around the hills and Nakki Lake. Being close to nature made the trip feel peaceful, and I really liked the place.

Photo: Mohit Aggarwal / Wikimedia Commons

MAHARASHTRA · MUMBAI

Mumbai, from an old memory

I went to Mumbai when I was very young, so I do not remember many details. It is still a place I would like to explore again one day.
